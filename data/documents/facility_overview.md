# CityPark Parking Facility — Overview

## About CityPark

CityPark is a modern, multi-storey car parking facility located in the heart of the city centre. The facility opened in 2018 and has been designed to provide a safe, convenient, and accessible parking experience for shoppers, commuters, tourists, and business visitors alike.

The building spans seven floors and provides a total of 850 parking spaces. The facility is operated 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with full security staff on site during peak hours.

## Capacity and Layout

- Total spaces: 850
- Standard spaces: 720
- Disabled (accessible) spaces: 50 (located on Ground Floor and Floor 1, near lifts)
- Electric vehicle (EV) charging spaces: 40 (located on Ground Floor and Floor 2)
- Motorcycle spaces: 20 (located on Ground Floor, Zone A)
- Oversized vehicle spaces: 20 (located on Floor 6, Zone C)

Each floor is clearly colour-coded and signposted to help drivers navigate. Lifts and stairwells are located at both ends of each floor.

## Security and Safety

CityPark is monitored by a 24-hour CCTV surveillance system covering all floors, stairwells, lifts, and entrance/exit points. The facility is patrolled by security staff from 07:00 to 23:00 every day. Emergency call points are installed every 30 metres throughout the facility.

The facility is well-lit with LED lighting on all floors, including at night. The entrance and exit barriers are automated and accept multiple payment methods.

## Accessibility

CityPark is fully accessible for visitors with disabilities. Features include:

- 50 designated disabled parking bays, all within 50 metres of a lift
- Step-free access from all floors via wide lifts (minimum 1.4 m x 2.1 m)
- Tactile floor markings guiding visitors from accessible bays to lifts
- Hearing loop systems in the pay station areas
- Accessible pay stations at lowered counter height

Blue Badge holders receive a discounted parking rate. Please display your Blue Badge clearly when parked in accessible bays.
