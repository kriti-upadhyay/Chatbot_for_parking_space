# CityPark — Rules, Policies, and Terms of Use

## General Rules

1. All drivers must comply with the Highway Code and any instructions given by CityPark staff or displayed on signage within the facility.
2. The speed limit within the facility is 5 mph (walking pace) at all times.
3. Drivers must give way to pedestrians at all times.
4. Mobile phone use while driving within the facility is strictly prohibited.
5. Horns must not be sounded except in an emergency.
6. Reversing into spaces is strongly recommended for safety and to assist exit.
7. Passengers must not be set down or collected in driving aisles.

## Parking Regulations

- Vehicles must be parked wholly within the marked bay lines.
- Double parking, obstructing aisles, or blocking other vehicles is not permitted.
- Vehicles must not be left in loading zones, disabled bays (without a valid Blue Badge), or reserved spaces without authorisation.
- Motorcycles must park in designated motorcycle bays only.
- Oversized vehicles (longer than 5.2 metres or wider than 2.2 metres) must use designated oversized spaces on Floor 6.
- Parking is not permitted on ramps, stairwells, or any area not designated as a parking bay.

## Reserved Spaces

A number of spaces are reserved for specific users (season ticket holders, business permit holders). These spaces are clearly marked. Parking in a reserved space without a valid permit will result in a Parking Charge Notice being issued.

## Disabled Parking

- Disabled bays are reserved exclusively for Blue Badge holders.
- The Blue Badge must be clearly displayed on the dashboard at all times.
- Misuse of disabled bays will result in a Parking Charge Notice.
- Blue Badge holders are entitled to a discounted rate.

## Electric Vehicle Charging

- EV charging bays are reserved for vehicles that are actively charging.
- Once charging is complete, the vehicle must be moved within 30 minutes.
- Charging cables must not be left trailing across walkways.
- Non-electric vehicles must not park in EV charging bays.

## Payment and Charges

- Parking charges apply 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including bank holidays.
- Payment must be made before leaving the facility at any pay station or via the CityPark mobile app.
- Failure to pay will result in a Parking Charge Notice.
- The grace period after payment is 15 minutes to allow the driver to return to their vehicle and exit.
- Lost ticket rates apply if the original entry ticket cannot be produced.

## Prohibited Items and Activities

The following are strictly prohibited within the facility:

- Storage of vehicles for more than 30 consecutive days without prior agreement.
- Carrying or storage of flammable liquids (other than fuel normally contained in the vehicle tank).
- Vehicle maintenance, repairs, or washing.
- Commercial activities without written permission.
- Camping or sleeping in vehicles overnight.
- Skateboarding, cycling, or use of scooters in pedestrian areas.

## CCTV and Data

The facility is monitored by CCTV. Recording is in place for security and operational purposes. Data is retained in accordance with applicable data protection legislation. Details of our data retention policy are available from customer services.

## Liability

CityPark accepts no liability for:
- Loss of or damage to any vehicle or its contents unless caused by our negligence.
- Loss or damage caused by fire, flood, or other events beyond our reasonable control.

Vehicles are parked entirely at the owner's risk. CityPark recommends that all vehicles are locked and valuables are not left on display.

## Parking Charge Notices

A Parking Charge Notice (PCN) of £85 (reduced to £50 if paid within 14 days) will be issued for any breach of these terms, including but not limited to: non-payment, overstaying, parking in unauthorised areas, or misuse of disabled bays.

Appeals against PCNs may be submitted within 28 days via the appeals process described on the notice.
