# CityPark — Facilities and Amenities

## Pay Stations

Pay stations are located on every floor near the lift lobbies, as well as at both pedestrian exits. They accept:

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, contactless)
- Cash (coins and notes; change given)
- CityPark app payment (scan QR code at pay station)
- CityPark pre-loaded account cards

Pay stations are available 24 hours a day. If a pay station is out of service, please use the nearest alternative or contact customer services.

## Mobile App

The CityPark mobile app (available on iOS and Android) allows you to:

- Pay for parking remotely
- Extend your parking session without returning to your vehicle
- Pre-book and manage reservations
- View real-time space availability
- Receive alerts when your parking session is about to expire
- Access receipts and payment history

Download the app from the App Store or Google Play. Existing customers can log in with their CityPark account credentials.

## Electric Vehicle Charging

CityPark offers 40 EV charging points:

- Ground Floor (Zone D): 16 x 7 kW AC Type 2 chargers
- Floor 2 (Zone B): 24 x 22 kW AC Type 2 chargers

All chargers are compatible with standard Type 2 connectors. Cables are provided at each bay. Payment for charging is separate from parking and is charged per kWh via the CityPark app or card at the charging unit. An EV charging session can be started and managed through the app.

## Lifts

Four lifts serve all seven floors. Lift lobbies are located at the north and south ends of each floor. Lifts are wide enough to accommodate wheelchairs and pushchairs. Lift dimensions: 1.4 m wide × 2.1 m deep.

In the event of a lift out of service, accessible stairways with handrails are available on both sides of the building. Please contact customer services if you require assistance.

## Toilets

Public toilets are located on the Ground Floor near the north lift lobby, and on Floor 3 near the south lift lobby.

- Ground Floor toilets include a fully accessible cubicle and baby-changing facilities.
- Floor 3 toilets include a unisex accessible cubicle.
- Toilets are open 06:00–23:00 daily.

## Baby-Changing Facilities

Baby-changing tables are available in the Ground Floor toilets (both male and female sections) and in the accessible toilet on Floor 3.

## Help Points and Security

Emergency help points are located throughout the facility. Pressing the button connects you directly to the security control room. Help points are marked with blue lighting and are located at every lift lobby and stairwell entrance on every floor.

Security staff are on patrol between 07:00 and 23:00. Outside these hours, the facility remains monitored by CCTV and a remote control room which can unlock help points and contact emergency services on your behalf.

## CCTV

Over 200 CCTV cameras monitor all floors, stairwells, lifts, and entrance/exit areas continuously. All footage is monitored in real time during staffed hours and stored for 31 days.

## Car Washing Service

A hand car wash service operates on Floor 1 (Zone A) Monday to Saturday, 08:00–17:00. Services include interior and exterior cleaning. Booking is not required; the service is first-come, first-served. Payment is made directly to the service operator.

## Bicycle Storage

Secure bicycle parking is provided free of charge on the Ground Floor (Zone E). There are 60 Sheffield-style cycle stands. A separate locked cage with 20 spaces is available for season ticket holders on request.

## Season Tickets and Monthly Permits

Season tickets offer significant savings for frequent users. Options include:

- Monthly (30-day rolling): Unlimited entry and exit during the permit period.
- Annual: 12-month permit with priority space guarantee.
- Business permit: For companies requiring multiple spaces; contact commercial@citypark.example.com.

Season tickets can be purchased and managed via the CityPark app or at customer services.

## Lost Property

Lost property found in the facility is held at the customer services desk on the Ground Floor for 28 days. After this period, unclaimed items are donated to charity. To enquire about lost property, call 0800 123 4568 or email lostandfound@citypark.example.com.
