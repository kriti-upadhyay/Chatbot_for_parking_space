# CityPark — Reservation Guide

## Why Reserve a Space?

Reserving a parking space in advance guarantees you a space upon arrival and can save you money compared to standard pay-on-arrival rates. Reserved spaces are held for the duration of your booking.

Pre-booking is especially recommended:
- During busy periods such as weekends, school holidays, and city events.
- When you have a fixed appointment or travel time.
- When you need an accessible or EV charging space.

## How to Make a Reservation

### Option 1: Chat Assistant (Recommended)

You can make a reservation directly through our chat assistant. The assistant will:
1. Ask for your first name and surname.
2. Ask for your vehicle registration number.
3. Ask for your preferred start date and time.
4. Ask for your preferred end date and time.
5. Confirm the details and calculate the cost.
6. Submit the reservation for administrator approval.

Once approved (typically within 15 minutes during staffed hours), you will receive a booking confirmation.

### Option 2: CityPark App

1. Download the CityPark app (iOS or Android).
2. Create an account or log in.
3. Tap "Reserve a Space".
4. Enter your vehicle registration, start date/time, and end date/time.
5. Select a space type (standard, disabled, EV charging, oversized).
6. Review the cost summary and confirm.
7. Payment is taken at the time of booking.

### Option 3: Website

Visit www.citypark.example.com/reserve. The booking process is the same as the app.

### Option 4: By Phone

Call customer services on 0800 123 4567. Reservations can be made by phone Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00.

## What Information Do I Need to Provide?

To complete a reservation you will need:

- **First name** and **surname** of the main driver.
- **Vehicle registration number** (e.g., AB12 CDE). This must match the vehicle that will be parked.
- **Start date and time**: When you intend to arrive.
- **End date and time**: When you intend to leave.

Please ensure the registration number is correct. The registration is used to verify your vehicle at the barrier. If the registration does not match, access may be refused.

## Reservation Lead Time

- Reservations can be made up to 90 days in advance.
- Reservations must be made at least 2 hours before the intended start time.
- Same-day reservations are subject to availability.

## Modifying a Reservation

Reservations can be modified up to 2 hours before the start time, subject to availability. To modify:
- Use the CityPark app: go to "My Bookings" and select "Modify".
- Call customer services: 0800 123 4567.

Changes to the start or end time may affect the total cost.

## Cancelling a Reservation

Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the start time receive a full refund. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the start time are non-refundable. To cancel:
- Use the CityPark app: go to "My Bookings" and select "Cancel".
- Call customer services: 0800 123 4567.

## Arriving with a Reservation

On arrival:
1. Drive to the entrance barrier.
2. Enter your booking reference number at the barrier keypad, or the barrier will read your number plate automatically if ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition) is enabled for your booking.
3. The barrier will raise. Drive to your reserved space (shown on your confirmation).
4. On exit, use the same booking reference or number plate recognition at the exit barrier. No additional payment is required if you leave within your booked time.

If you arrive outside your booked time window, the barrier may not recognise your booking automatically. Press the help button for assistance.

## Overstaying Your Reservation

If you stay beyond your booked end time, standard hourly rates apply for the additional time. Payment for the overstay must be made at a pay station before exiting.

## Confirmation and Receipts

A booking confirmation is sent by email immediately after booking. Receipts are available in the CityPark app under "Payment History".
