# CityPark — Location and Directions

## Address

CityPark Multi-Storey Car Park
14 Central Plaza
City Centre
CC1 2AB

## Getting Here by Car

### From the North (Motorway M1)
Leave the motorway at Junction 12 and follow signs for "City Centre". At the third roundabout take the second exit onto Ring Road North. Continue for 1.2 miles. Turn left at the traffic lights onto Central Avenue. CityPark is 400 metres on your right, clearly signposted.

### From the South (Motorway M6)
Leave the motorway at Junction 8 and follow signs for "City Centre Parking". Take the A420 northbound for 2 miles. At the large junction with the flyover, go straight ahead onto Central Boulevard. Turn right onto Station Road, then left onto Central Plaza. The entrance to CityPark is on your left.

### From the East (A38)
Follow the A38 westbound into the city. At the civic centre junction, bear left onto City Road. Continue past the main shopping centre and turn left at the second set of traffic lights onto Central Plaza. CityPark entrance is 200 metres ahead on the right.

### From the West (Ring Road)
Follow the Ring Road eastbound and take the exit signed "City Centre / Parking". Follow brown car park signs through two sets of traffic lights. Turn right onto Central Plaza. CityPark is on the left side.

## Nearby Landmarks

- The facility is directly opposite the Central Library.
- It is a 3-minute walk from the City Centre Bus Station.
- The entrance is 150 metres from the main shopping centre (Westgate Mall).
- The train station is a 7-minute walk via Station Road.

## Public Transport Alternatives

If you prefer not to drive, excellent public transport links are available:

- **Bus**: Routes 12, 14, 22, and 45 stop at "Central Plaza" bus stop directly outside.
- **Train**: City Centre Station is a 7-minute walk. National Rail services connect to major cities.
- **Tram**: The city tram network stops at "City Central" stop, a 4-minute walk.
- **Cycle**: Secure bicycle parking is available free of charge on the ground floor.

## Entrance and Exit Points

The main vehicle entrance is on Central Plaza (north side of the building). Exit barriers are on the south side of the building, leading onto Station Road. Vehicles must use the designated entrance and exit lanes at all times. The entrance is wide enough for vehicles up to 2.1 metres in height and 2.3 metres in width.

**Maximum vehicle height: 2.1 metres.** Vehicles exceeding this height cannot enter the facility.

## Pedestrian Access

Pedestrian entrances are located at:
- North side: Central Plaza (adjacent to the vehicle entrance)
- South side: Station Road (open 06:00–23:00)
- East side: Arcade Lane (open 07:00–22:00)
