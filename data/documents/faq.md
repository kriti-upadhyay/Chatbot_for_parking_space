# CityPark — Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

**Q: Is CityPark open 24 hours?**
A: Yes, CityPark is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including bank holidays. Security staff are on site from 07:00 to 23:00. Outside these hours, the facility is monitored remotely by CCTV and help points remain active.

**Q: What is the maximum vehicle height?**
A: The maximum vehicle height is 2.1 metres. Vehicles taller than this cannot enter the facility. If you have a tall vehicle (e.g., a van, campervan, or vehicle with roof bars/bike rack), please check your vehicle height before entering.

**Q: Are motorcycles allowed?**
A: Yes. Motorcycles and scooters must park in the designated motorcycle bays on the Ground Floor, Zone A. Motorcycle parking is charged at a reduced rate.

**Q: Can I stay overnight?**
A: Overnight parking is permitted. Standard hourly or daily rates apply. However, sleeping in vehicles overnight is not permitted under the facility rules.

**Q: What happens if I lose my ticket?**
A: If you lose your entry ticket, please go to the customer services desk on the Ground Floor. A lost ticket fee applies (equivalent to 24 hours of standard parking). Please bring your vehicle registration details to assist with verification.

**Q: Is the car park suitable for large vans?**
A: Standard bays accommodate vehicles up to 5.2 metres long and 2.2 metres wide. Longer or wider vehicles should use the oversized spaces on Floor 6 (Zone C). All vehicles must be under 2.1 metres tall to enter.

## Payment Questions

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Pay stations accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), contactless, cash, and the CityPark app. Pre-loaded CityPark account cards are also accepted. Receipts are available from pay stations and via the app.

**Q: Can I pay using my phone?**
A: Yes. The CityPark app allows you to pay for parking, extend sessions, and manage reservations. Alternatively, Apple Pay and Google Pay are accepted at all card-capable pay stations.

**Q: What if the pay station is not working?**
A: If a pay station is out of service, please use the nearest working pay station or pay via the CityPark app. If you experience difficulty, press the help button near any pay station to speak to staff.

**Q: Can I get a VAT receipt?**
A: Yes. VAT receipts are available from any pay station or from the CityPark app under "Payment History".

## Reservation Questions

**Q: How far in advance can I reserve a space?**
A: Reservations can be made up to 90 days in advance. Same-day reservations must be made at least 2 hours before your intended arrival time.

**Q: What if I arrive earlier or later than my reservation?**
A: If you arrive early, standard pay-on-arrival rates will apply until your booking starts. If you arrive late (after your booking start time), your reserved space will be held for up to 30 minutes. After 30 minutes, the space may be released. Contact customer services if you are running significantly late.

**Q: Can I amend my reservation?**
A: Yes, reservations can be amended up to 2 hours before the start time, subject to availability. Amendments can be made via the app or by calling customer services.

**Q: What if I need to cancel?**
A: Cancellations more than 24 hours before start time receive a full refund. Within 24 hours, cancellations are non-refundable.

## Disabled Access Questions

**Q: Are there accessible parking bays?**
A: Yes. CityPark has 50 accessible bays designated for Blue Badge holders, located on the Ground Floor and Floor 1, within 50 metres of a lift.

**Q: Do Blue Badge holders pay the standard rate?**
A: Blue Badge holders are entitled to a discounted rate. Please display your Blue Badge clearly and mention it when paying or reserving.

**Q: What if I need assistance?**
A: Press any help point button or call customer services on 0800 123 4567. Staff will be happy to assist with navigation, payments, or any other need.

## EV Charging Questions

**Q: Where are the EV charging points?**
A: EV charging points are on the Ground Floor (Zone D, 16 chargers) and Floor 2 (Zone B, 24 chargers). All use the standard Type 2 connector and cables are provided.

**Q: How do I pay for EV charging?**
A: Charging is paid separately from parking. Use the CityPark app or tap your card at the charging unit. Charging is billed per kWh.

**Q: How long can I stay in an EV bay?**
A: You must move your vehicle within 30 minutes of your charging session completing. Remaining in an EV bay after charging finishes may result in a Parking Charge Notice.

## Safety and Security

**Q: Is the car park safe?**
A: CityPark has a comprehensive CCTV system with over 200 cameras, emergency help points on every floor, and security staff on patrol from 07:00 to 23:00. The facility is well-lit with LED lighting throughout. We recommend locking your vehicle and not leaving valuables on display.

**Q: What do I do in an emergency?**
A: Press the nearest emergency help point button (marked in blue) to speak directly to the security control room. Alternatively, call 999 for the emergency services.

**Q: Is there fire safety provision?**
A: Yes. The facility has full fire detection and suppression systems, clearly marked fire exits, and emergency lighting. Fire evacuation procedures are posted on every floor.
